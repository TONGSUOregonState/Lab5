2.0 Background

In the “week 4 Lab 3 – Measuring Impedance” you used your oscilloscope and signal generator to determine the impedance of a known component by determining the current phasor and applying Ohm’s law.    This lab is a logical extension where the component type and value are unknown.    You will use a similar technique to that employed in lab 3 to determine the impedance, and from that find the component type and value.

As the experimental technique and many of the observations are similar to lab 3, I recommend you have a copy of your lab report available to you as you execute this lab.

In general, the values of components of a linear network which produce a specific impedance at a specific frequency are not unique.    However, for a single component they are theoretically unique, and you can utilize a single frequency.    In practice you should ensure that the frequency you utilize produces a meaningful measurement and utilize multiple frequencies to verify your result.

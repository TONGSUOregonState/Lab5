Use your analytical results from section 3.1 and your experimental results to determine the contents of Mystery Box #1.   You will need to plug your measured results into the magnitude equation or the phase equation and solve for C, L, or R (One of them is a lot easier mathematically).    Once you find C, L, or R back substitute your result into the whichever of the magnitude or phase equations you didn’t use and verify the result against your measurement.    For example, if you have a capacitor and you used the phase equation to determine C, then plug C back into magnitude equation and verify that matches your measured result.   Show your work.

**Response / evidence:**

For a series resistor R and a capacitor with the output measured across the capacitor,

H(jω) = Vz/Vs = 1/(1 + jωRC),

|H| = 1/sqrt(1 + (ωRC)^2), and arg(H) = −atan(ωRC).

Using the measured magnitude ratio g = 0.897164/1.948 = 0.460556 at f ≈ 1000 Hz:

RC = sqrt(g^(−2) − 1)/(2πf) = **306.739 µs**.

Thus **C = (306.739 µs)/R**, with R in ohms giving C in farads. If the actual external series resistance is confirmed as 1.000 kΩ, this gives **C ≈ 306.7 nF**. This numerical capacitance is conditional, not a verified component value; the resistance has not been independently confirmed in the available record. BOX ID: **1-ZFR**.

Independent phase back-substitution gives φpred = −atan(2πfRC) = **−62.58°**, compared with **−66.55°** from screenshot 1, a difference of **3.97°**. The results indicate capacitive behavior but are not an exact magnitude/phase match.
